
* Crystal caves *

*ENGLISH*
*********

Let the colorful lights of the crystal caves welcome you!

********

v1.1 2025/10/20
Added sounds, music and time trial times!

********

Author : Killer Wheels
Tools used : 3ds max, photoshop, makeitgood, RV Exporter for 3ds max
Creation time : way too much...again


********

This track features a "Reverse" mode, cameras for the "Replay", time split for time trial and a practice star.
Requires RVGL! (May work in rv1.2 but I didn't test)
Start grid support for 16 players.

********

Thanks to all who played and who are still playing, for their creations and help within Re-volt community

********

Have fun ! :)
